Beginnix roadmap

Puzzle track
------------
The puzzle track starts at challenge 34. The tools shown here describe
possible solution paths. Learners choose their own commands.

Live puzzles

34  Dispatch packet         MULTI-TOOL   grep, find, cp, printf, diff
35  Intake reconciliation   MULTI-TOOL   grep, cut, sort, comm
36  Verified archive note   MULTI-TOOL   tar, grep, cp, printf
37  Latest status audit     MULTI-TOOL   awk, sort
38  Timeline correlation    MULTI-TOOL   awk, sort
39  Approved file access     MULTI-TOOL   awk, Bash while/read, chmod, stat
40  Overnight SUID audit     MULTI-TOOL   diff, awk
41  Repair a rejected manifest  MULTI-TOOL   cat -A, sed, cmp

Planned pairs

42–43  Processes, services, and logs
  Reconcile a process inventory with captured status reports.
  MULTI-TOOL  ps, awk, sort, diff
  Diagnose a stopped service from a supplied status capture, journal, and config revisions.
  Grade the first causal error and a repaired local config copy.
  MULTI-TOOL  systemctl, journalctl, grep
  Add focused practice with systemctl and journalctl before this puzzle.

44–45  Storage and local shares
  Match devices to mount points and verify a storage report.
  MULTI-TOOL  lsblk, findmnt, df
  Reconcile a captured SMB share inventory with a local manifest.
  MULTI-TOOL  smbclient, sort, diff
  Add focused practice with lsblk, findmnt, and smbclient before these
  puzzles. Supply offline examples of share listings. Setup requires no live share.

46–47  Files and archives
  Verify a tree of symbolic links, hard links, and copies.
  MULTI-TOOL  find, readlink, stat, diff
  Recover the right revision across several compressed bundles.
  MULTI-TOOL  file, tar, gzip, grep, diff

Additional puzzle queue

• Who was approved to do what? Compare a fictional sudo -l transcript with
  the role and ticket expiry. Identify the account, excess grant, evidence
  and intended scope. No host policy is consulted or modified.
• Where did the disk space go? Inspect a small sparse file, two hard links,
  and an ordinary payload. Identify the shared inode and which removal would
  reclaim the most storage. Grade relationships rather than fixed block counts.
• Recover one lost change with Git. Restore a route entry in a small offline
  repository while preserving a newer change and an unrelated working edit.
• Why did dispatch stop? Use the captured service investigation in the
  planned 42–43 pair. Include a later distracting warning; verify the cause
  and corrected config without starting a service.

Future course sections
Setup now offers guided training, puzzles, a direct jump, and checkpoint
restore. Add more focused tracks when there is enough material. Possible
tracks include system administration, permissions, and privilege-escalation
concepts. Save longer challenges that combine several tools for the
advanced tracks.
